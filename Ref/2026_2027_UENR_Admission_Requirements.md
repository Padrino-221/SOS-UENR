## 1. General Information About the University

The University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR) was established by an Act of Parliament, University of Energy and Natural Resources Act, 2011 (Act 830). The University is a public funded institution of higher learning and research with an additional mandate to provide leadership in the sciences, engineering, technology and management of energy and natural resources. The overall aim is to become a centre of excellence in these critical areas.

The University approaches its programmes and research with strong emphasis on interdisciplinary collaboration in the areas of economics, law and policy, management, science, technology, and engineering as well as socio-political issues that affect energy and the management of natural resources. These objectives are pursued through student-centred didactic methods in research, teaching, and learning. The University admitted its pioneering students of 154 in 2012 and currently has over 18,000 enrolled students pursuing various diploma, undergraduate, and graduate programmes at the Sunyani and Dormaa campuses.

### 1.1 Vision Statement
To become a world-class institution for generating, advancing, and applying knowledge in energy and natural resource sciences.

### 1.2 Mission Statement
To promote the development of human resources and skills required to solve critical energy and natural resources challenges of society and undertake interdisciplinary academic, research, and outreach programmes in engineering, science, economics, and environmental policy.

### 1.3 Core Values
Guided by core values exemplified by staff, students, and alumni to realize its vision and mission: Knowledge, Integrity, Impact.

### 1.6 General Entry Requirements

#### 1.6.1 Regular Applicants

##### (a) 4-Year Degree Programmes
* **SSSCE / WASSCE:** Credit passes (A1–C6 / A–D respectively) in three (3) core subjects: English Language, Mathematics, and Integrated Science/Social Studies, and three (3) elective subjects relevant to the programme of study.
* **General Business Certificate Examination (GBCE):** Credit passes (A–D) in six (6) subjects comprising three core subjects including English Language and Mathematics plus three (3) relevant elective subjects.
* **Advanced Business Certificate Examination (ABCE):** Full diploma certificate in ABCE. Must have credit passes in five (5) subjects including 3 compulsory subjects (English Language, Mathematics, Integrated Science or Social Studies) in GBCE/SSCE/WASSCE plus two (2) optional subjects relevant to the programme.
* **GCE "A" Level:** Passes in three (3) subjects (at least one pass should be D or better). Credit passes (Grade 6) in five GCE "O" Level subjects including English, Mathematics, and a Science (for non-science students) / Arts subject (for science students).
* **Higher National Diploma (HND) Holders:** Must have graduated with a good HND certificate in a relevant discipline with relevant working experience. May be required to write an entrance examination and/or interview.
* **Diploma Holders:** UENR Diplomas or recognized equivalents with relevant working experience. Admitted to Level 200 or Level 300 based on FGPA and area of study.
* **Other / Foreign Qualifications:**
  - **International Baccalaureate (IB) / Cambridge IGCSE / 'A' Levels:** Three relevant subjects at 'A' Level plus at least five (5) passes including English and Mathematics at 'O' Level/IGCSE.
  - **American Grade 12 Examinations.**
  - **Note:** International/Foreign certificates must be submitted to the Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC) for evaluation.

##### (b) 2-Year Diploma Programmes
* **SSSCE / WASSCE:** Credit passes (A1–C6) in English Language, Mathematics, and Integrated Science/Social Studies plus two credit passes (A1–C6) in two elective subjects relevant to the programme.

#### 1.6.2 Mature Applicants
* Must be at least 25 years old (proof of age via birth certificate required).
* Must have at least five (5) years working experience in the relevant discipline.
* SSSCE: At least E in 6 subjects (3 core including English and Mathematics + 3 electives).
* WASSCE: At least E8 in 6 subjects (3 core including English and Mathematics + 3 electives).
* Must pass Mature Students' Entrance Examinations conducted by UENR (English Language, Mathematics, and Aptitude Test).

##### 1.6.2.1 Preparatory Classes / Entrance Examination for Mature Applicants
Applicants may be invited to mandatory preparatory classes before taking the entrance exam. Exam date will be communicated to all registered applicants.
