# WASSCE Senior High School Programme & Subject Breakdown

## 1. Core WASSCE Subjects
Every school candidate takes **4 Core Subjects**:
- English Language
- Core Mathematics
- Integrated Science
- Social Studies

*Note: WAEC guidance requires candidates to have Continuous Assessment (CASS) records in 7–8 subjects (4 Core + 3 or 4 Electives).*

---

## 2. General Science Electives
Common elective subjects include:
- Elective Mathematics
- Biology
- Chemistry
- Physics
- Geography
- General Agriculture
- ICT (Elective)
- French
- Music

*Requirement: General Science candidates take Elective Mathematics plus two or three subjects from the options listed above.*

---
